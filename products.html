Fresh Fish

Our fish counter changes daily based on what's freshest at the markets. Below are our regulars — plus whatever the sea has offered up that morning.

  • Yellowfin Tuna

    South Australian caught. Exceptional sashimi-grade flesh. Available whole or filleted to order.

  • Atlantic Salmon

    Premium quality, consistently fresh. Ideal for everyday cooking or sushi-grade preparations.

  • Kingfish (Yellowtail)

    Prized for its rich, buttery flavour. A South Australian favourite, filleted fresh in-store.

  • Smoked Fish Range

    A selection of house-smoked fish — ask the team what's on today.

  • Game Fish

    Marlin, broadbill and seasonal game fish when available. Ask in-store.

  • Daily Market Catch

    Whole fish and fillets direct from the morning market — the most seasonal, the most fresh.

Shellfish & Crustaceans

From live South Australian crayfish to king prawns and everything in between — our shellfish range is as comprehensive as it gets for a local fishmonger.

  • Live SA Crayfish

    Southern rock lobster, sourced live from South Australian waters. Popular for Christmas and special occasions.

  • Fresh Crab

    Blue swimmer and mud crab when available. Sold whole or cleaned. Ask the team.

  • King Prawns

    Fresh and selected frozen. Green or cooked. Available by weight.

  • Scallops

    Saucer scallops, roe-on when available. Fresh or cleaned to order.

  • Mussels & Cockles

    South Australian mussels, live or pre-cleaned. Cockles fresh from the market.

  • Squid

    Whole or cleaned tubes. Great for calamari, barbecue, or stuffing.

Oysters

Our oysters are shucked fresh in-store, year-round — never pre-shucked, never sitting. We take oysters seriously.

  • Natural Oysters

    Freshly shucked on the half shell. Clean, briny, and ready to eat. By the half dozen, dozen or more.

  • Kilpatrick Oysters

    Our house Kilpatrick sauce, baked to order. A crowd favourite. By the half dozen or dozen.

  • Oyster Flight Tastings

    A curated selection of oyster styles for events and special occasions. By request — call us to arrange.

  • Event & Function Platters

    Large-format oyster platters on ice for functions, parties and corporate events. Contact us to discuss quantities.
